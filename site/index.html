It is rewriting, in real time, the assumptions we inherited about work, learning, and thought — faster than we can renegotiate them.

Such an era does not reward faster reactions. It demands deeper ones: a reconsideration of what we build, what we keep, and what we are for.

This site is being rebuilt around that question.
